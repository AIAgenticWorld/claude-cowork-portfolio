### Engagement Recommendations

**Opening Hook**

"I checked where Peeplcoach comes up when a CHRO types 'executive coaching platform' into ChatGPT — BetterUp and CoachHub appear immediately, Peeplcoach doesn't. Given the strength of your coach roster, that's a positioning gap, not a product gap. That's what I wanted to talk about."

**Lead With This Pain Point**

AI search invisibility — enterprise HR buyers now shortlist vendors by asking ChatGPT before visiting websites. Not appearing means losing deals before the first conversation.

**Value Proposition (One Sentence)**

We make Peeplcoach the platform that shows up when CHROs and L&D heads ask AI what the best coaching options are — before they ever reach out to BetterUp.

**Social Proof to Use**

AI visibility audit results for a mid-market HR tech client — we moved them from zero mentions to consistent top-3 placement in ChatGPT and Perplexity for their category keywords within 90 days.

**Call-to-Action Strategy**

- Immediate next step: Share a 2-slide AI visibility audit showing exactly where Peeplcoach appears (and doesn't) vs BetterUp and CoachHub
- Timing: Tuesday or Wednesday morning based on LinkedIn posting pattern
- Urgency driver: BetterUp is actively running AI content campaigns right now — the gap will widen each month

**Multi-Touch Outreach Sequence**

1. LinkedIn connection (Day 1): "Deeksha — I run AI visibility audits for HR tech platforms. Did a quick check on Peeplcoach vs BetterUp in ChatGPT results and found something worth a 5-minute conversation. Mind if I share?"

2. Email follow-up (Day 3): Subject: "Peeplcoach vs BetterUp — AI search gap" | Lead with: the specific query result showing where each platform appears

3. Phone call (Day 7): "Hi Deeksha, [Persona] here — I sent you a note on LinkedIn and email about the AI search audit for Peeplcoach. Two minutes?"

4. Video message (Day 10): Screen recording showing a CHRO typing "executive coaching platform" into ChatGPT and the results page — Peeplcoach not visible. No voiceover needed — the visual makes the point.

**Objection Pre-emption**

Objection 1 — "We don't have budget for this right now":
Counter: "The audit itself takes a week and shows exactly which queries you're missing. The investment decision comes after you see the data — not before. Would it help to start with just the audit?"

Objection 2 — "We tried SEO before and it didn't move the needle":
Counter: "Traditional SEO optimises for Google's algorithm. AI search works differently — it's about entity authority, citation coverage, and structured content that AI models pull from. Different inputs, different results. The audit shows which specific gaps matter."

**Stakeholder Expansion**

If Deeksha likes the concept, expect her to loop in a co-founder or marketing lead before committing. Prepare a one-pager version of the audit that a non-technical stakeholder can understand at a glance.
